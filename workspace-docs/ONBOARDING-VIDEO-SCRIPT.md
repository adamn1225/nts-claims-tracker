# NTS Claims Tracker — Onboarding Video Script

**Audience:** New brokers / internal team members  
**Estimated runtime:** ~2–2.5 minutes at a comfortable pace  
*(Trim the Broker Tools section to keep it under 90 seconds)*

---

## [OPENING — show dashboard home screen]

"Hey team, welcome — this is a quick walkthrough of the NTS Claims Tracker, the CRM built specifically for our brokerage. Whether you're brand new or just getting familiar, this covers everything you need to hit the ground running."

---

## [INTENTION — pan across the dashboard]

"The whole point of this tool is simple: stop losing deals to sticky notes and missed follow-ups. Every customer, every conversation, and every task you need to action lives in one place — accessible from your desktop or your phone."

---

## [KANBAN BOARD — navigate to /dashboard/customers/kanban]

"This is your Book of Business — the Kanban board. Each card is a customer or prospect, and the columns represent where they are in your pipeline: Prospect, Active, Won, or Lost.

You can drag cards between stages as deals progress. Click any card to see the full customer profile — contact info, shipping frequency, industry, and a complete log of every interaction you've had with them.

Use the pin icon to keep your highest-priority customers pinned to the top so they're always front and center."

---

## [TASKS & FOLLOW-UPS — navigate to /dashboard/tasks or calendar]

"Next up is your task and follow-up system. Every customer should have a next action scheduled — a call, an email, a check-in. You'll see overdue items flagged in red so nothing falls through the cracks.

The calendar view gives you a visual timeline of what's coming up across your book."

---

## [BROKER TOOLS — navigate to /dashboard/broker-tools]

"We've also built out a set of tools to help you close more freight. The Carrier Pay Estimator pulls live market rate data so you know what carriers expect on any lane. The Carrier Finder helps you source the right carrier by equipment type and lane history. And the Lane Launchpad gives you a full compliance brief for oversize loads — permits, state requirements, all of it."

---

## [CLOSING — return to dashboard]

"That's the core of it. The goal is to keep you focused on your customers and your deals — not on chasing down information. If you have questions, reach out to your manager or admin. Now go close some freight."
