# Communication Libraries (Expanded)

## Core Principles

* Always end with a CTA and at least one question.  
  Example: “Would you like me to send paperwork today, or set up a quick call?”

* Match brand tone:

  * TT → Warm, farmer-friendly, conversational

  * CT → Direct, efficient, systematic

  * HH → Industrial, expert, structured

  * HET → Professional, personalized, authoritative

  * AT → Consumer-friendly, reassuring

  * NTS → Brand-heavy, national credibility

  * WLS → Technical, compliance-driven

* Subject Lines: Include brand marker (e.g., \[HET\] Shipping Estimate Follow-Up).

* Formatting:

  * HH/HET/CT/NTS/WLS → Tables, HTML, structured layouts allowed

  * TT → Plain text, no tables or HTML

  * AT → Simple, polished consumer style

* Transactional first: Emails and SMS prioritize moving the booking forward with specific details, not generic marketing.

## Follow-Up Cadence Examples

* **Day 0 – Immediate Response:** Confirm specs needed, thank for inquiry, invite to share missing info.

* **Day 1–2 – Quote Delivery:** Structured breakdown (HH/HET), conversational (TT), ticket-style (CT), reassurance (AT).

* **Day 3–5 – Second Touch:** Ask if specs changed, offer re-quote, emphasize urgency (capacity, permits).

* **Day 7–10 – Final Attempt:** Short, direct: “Would you like us to keep this file open?”

## Objection Handling Email Snippets

* **Price Too High (HH/HET/NTS):** “Other quotes may not include permits, escorts, or vetted carriers. We make sure your move is legal and safe — avoiding costly delays. Would you like me to rework timing or equipment to bring this within range?”

* **Cheaper Competitor (CT/AT):** “Often cheaper bids skip insurance or underbid drivers. We secure fully licensed carriers so your load isn’t stranded. Would you like me to match timing with a vetted option?”

* **Timing Unclear (WLS):** “Permit approval can take 3–5 business days depending on the state. If we start today, we can target \[date\]. Should I move forward with applications?”

* **DIY Move (TT/HH):** “For tractors/containers, permits, escorts, and liability fall on you if self-moved. With us, compliance and safety are handled. Want me to show the total cost difference?”

* **Delivered:** “Delivery complete. Thank you for choosing \[Brand\]. Would you like me to assist with your next shipment?”

## Internal Announcements (Tone)

* **Company-Wide:** Concise, direct, professional. No thank-you fluff.

* **Brand Marketing Updates:** Use brand voice even internally (CT transactional, TT warm, etc.).

* **Performance Notices:** Fact-based, stats-driven (e.g., “Top brokers receive $7,500–$10,000 in weekly leads by logging in daily.”).
