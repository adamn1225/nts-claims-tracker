NTS Sales & Marketing Playbook
===============================\
✅ Purpose\
To train and empower both human sales reps and AI agents (Sales Agent, Email Copilot, and CMO Image Generator) with structured sales frameworks, objection handling, and outreach material tailored to NTS Logistics and its brand family.

Sales Framework Overview
------------------------

### Framework 1 -- Keeping It Simple (Entry-Level Cold Call)

Goal: Get new reps or AI trainees comfortable with opening lines. Tone: Friendly, non-technical.\
Structure: Simple intros → Branch into freight/no-freight paths.\
Use: Training or AI 'light mode' outreach.

### Framework 2 -- Cold Call Cheat Sheet (Intermediate)

Goal: Provide repeatable cold call rhythm.\
Structure: Intro → Hook → Value → Engagement → Close.\
Use: Core script for NTS Sales Agent and reps.

### Framework 3 -- Version B (Closer Style)

Goal: Equip advanced reps to convert opportunities into test loads.\
Tone: Confident, assumes freight exists.\
Use: Follow-up or advanced calls.

### Framework 4 -- Mock Call Scenarios (Roleplay)

Goal: Train tone and objection handling.\
Use: Training sessions or AI fine-tuning.
