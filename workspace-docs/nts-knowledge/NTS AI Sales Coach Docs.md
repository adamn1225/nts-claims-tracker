This builds trust and often wins over clients who value transparency and professionalism.

   * **Prospect by Industry:** Identify industries that rely on logistics (construction, agriculture, energy, etc.) and **reach out to companies in those fields**. Use tools (like LinkedIn or ZoomInfo) to get in touch with the right person (e.g., logistics coordinator, equipment manager).

   * **Don’t Wait – Take Action:** The overarching theme is to **be proactive**. Brokers who consistently call, email, and network (even when inbound leads are slow) build a pipeline that others miss.
